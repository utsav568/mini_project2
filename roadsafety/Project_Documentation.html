1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

In trauma and emergency medicine, the "Golden Hour" dictates that medical intervention within the first 60 minutes after an accident drastically reduces mortality and long-term morbidity. However, municipal dispatch control centers suffer from severe operational friction:

  • Inaccurate Witness Triage: Distressed callers provide fragmented or panicked accounts, causing dispatchers to misjudge crash severity or send inadequate resources.
  • Static or Suboptimal Navigation: Conventional GPS tools do not account for vehicle-specific physical road constraints (e.g. heavy fire tenders cannot navigate narrow service alleys).
  • Communication Blackouts: Highway accidents or major disasters frequently disable cellular towers, preventing casualty telemetry from reaching the hospital network.

GuardianNodes RoadSoS solves this with an integrated Computer-Aided Dispatch (CAD) system combining Natural Language Processing, dynamic graph algorithms, and decentralized vehicular ad-hoc networks.

2. System Architecture & Key Modules

🧠 A. Multi-Modal Intelligent Triage Engine

Performs instant semantic analysis on witness statements and fused multi-modal evidence tags:

  • Zero-Shot NLP Classification: Classifies incidents into 5 emergency categories: Fire/Explosion, Critical Medical Trauma, Minor Collision, Hazardous Material Leak, and Vehicle Entrapment.
  • Entity Extraction (NER): Identifies vehicle quantities, casualty numbers, fire mentions, hazardous substances, and entrapment indicators via compiled regular expressions.
  • Negation Handling: Parses semantic negations (e.g., "no injuries reported" or "zero casualties") to prevent high-urgency false alarms.
  • Mathematical Urgency Index: Calculates a continuous priority index bounded in [1.00, 10.00] using a sigmoid activation function:
Base_Score = Σ (Classification_Score_i × Severity_Weight_i)
Raw_Urgency = Sigmoid(Base_Score × 6.0 - 3.0) × 9.0 + 1.0
Final_Urgency = Clamp(Raw_Urgency + Casualty_Bonus + Hazard_Bonus, 1.00, 10.00)
  • Golden Hour SLA Window: Maps urgency to strict response deadlines: Critical (≤ 8 min), High (≤ 12 min), Moderate (≤ 20 min).

🗺️ B. Dynamic Traffic-Aware Dijkstra Routing Engine

Builds and maintains a synthetic municipal directed road network mapped to Delhi NCR landmarks:

  • Network Topology: 10×10 directed graph (100 nodes, ~180 directed edges) with highway, arterial, local, and service corridors.
  • GPS Nearest-Node Snapping: Computes Euclidean/Haversine distance to map raw witness GPS coordinates (lat, lon) to the nearest road intersection.
  • Vehicle-Specific Cost Function: Different response vehicles (Fire Tenders, CATS Ambulances, Highway Patrol, HazMat Units, Motorbike Medics) experience specialized road penalties:
Edge_Weight = Base_Distance × Road_Type_Multiplier × Traffic_Congestion_Multiplier × Vehicle_Penalty
  • Two-Leg Coordination: Simultaneously plots the primary route (Depot → Crash Site) and the secondary evacuation route (Crash Site → Nearest Capable Trauma Center).

📡 C. Decentralized BLE / V2X Mesh Network Simulator

Ensures continuous telemetry flow even when cellular 4G/5G connectivity is completely offline:

  • Compact 24-Byte Binary Frame: Packed using Python struct format !fffIHBB: Latitude (4B), Longitude (4B), Urgency (4B), Timestamp (4B), Sequence (2B), Flags (1B), Reserved (1B), and CRC-32 (4B).
  • Multi-Hop Vehicular Ad-Hoc Network (VANET): Relays packets through passing ambulances, police patrol cars, transit buses, and smart Roadside Units (RSUs).
  • Integrity Verification & Healing: Every hop validates the 32-bit CRC checksum. If bit-flips or RF packet loss occur, up to 3 automatic retransmissions are triggered.
  • Common Alerting Protocol (CAP): Produces OASIS / ITU-T X.1303 compliant JSON alerts for national emergency systems (ERSS 112).

🏥 D. Regional Trauma Center & Hospital Readiness Fleet

Coordinates live resource matching with premier tertiary trauma hospitals in Delhi NCR:

  • AIIMS Apex Trauma Centre: Level-1 Apex Trauma Institute (Specialized Neuro & Polytrauma).
  • Safdarjung Hospital Emergency Block: Level-1 Specialized Burns & Critical Trauma.
  • Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia (RML) Hospital: Level-2 Central Regional Facility.
  • Max Super Speciality Saket: Level-1 Advanced Cardiac & Neuro Facility.
  • Tracks available ICU beds, active trauma bays, blood bank supplies, and direct dispatch hotlines.

3. Technology Stack

  • Frontend & UI: Streamlit 1.67 featuring responsive dark glassmorphism design.
  • Runtime Environment: Python 3.14 (64-bit) with localized isolated virtual environment.
  • Graph & Pathfinding Algorithms: NetworkX 3.7 (Dijkstra Shortest Path, Single-Source Dijkstra).
  • AI & Natural Language Processing: HuggingFace Transformers, Regex Semantic Tokenizer.
  • Data Visualization: Plotly 7.1 (Dark Scattermapbox, Polar Radar Charts, Spline Telemetry).
  • Networking & Serialization: Python Struct (Binary Packing), Binascii (CRC-32 Checksum), JSON.
  • Automated Export Suite: FPDF (Automated PDF Report), Python-PPTX (Automated Slide Presentation).
